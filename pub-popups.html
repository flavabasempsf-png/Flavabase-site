HOW IT WORKS

A Simple Partnership That Works

We make running food nights easy. Our team arrives, sets up our professional catering unit, prepares everything fresh and serves your customers while your staff focus on the bar.

There is minimal disruption to your venue and no need for kitchen facilities. We bring everything required for a smooth and professional service.

At the end of the evening we pack away, leaving your venue clean and ready for the next day.
