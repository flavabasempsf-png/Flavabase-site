OUR STORY

Built on Passion. Driven by Family.

Every dish we serve is inspired by generations of Punjabi cooking, bringing authentic flavours and warm hospitality to every event.

FROM FAMILY KITCHEN

From Family Kitchen to Flava Base

Flava Base began with a simple idea: share the flavours that brought our own family together with people celebrating their most important moments.

Inspired by traditional Punjabi recipes, our menu blends authentic cooking with a modern street food experience that's perfect for weddings, corporate events, festivals and private celebrations.

Every meal is prepared with care, quality ingredients and the same pride that has always been at the heart of our family kitchen.

MORE THAN GREAT FOOD

Bringing People Together

Food has always been at the centre of Punjabi culture. It's how we celebrate milestones, welcome guests and bring families together.

At Flava Base, we believe every event deserves freshly prepared food served with genuine warmth, creating memorable experiences for every guest.

Whether we're catering for an intimate family gathering or serving hundreds of guests at a wedding or corporate event, our goal remains exactly the same — deliver authentic Punjabi flavours with exceptional service.

IN LOVING MEMORY

The Heart Behind Flava Base

Flava Base is dedicated to the loving memory of my mum, whose passion for Punjabi cooking continues to inspire every dish we prepare today.

Her recipes, generosity and love for bringing people together remain at the heart of everything we do. Every meal we serve is a tribute to the values she passed on to our family.
