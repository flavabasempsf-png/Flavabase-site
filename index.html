OUR STORY

Built on Passion. Driven by Family.

Flava Base was created from a lifelong passion for Punjabi cooking, inspired by treasured family recipes that have been passed down through generations.

Every event we cater reflects the warmth, hospitality and authentic flavours of Punjab, served with a modern street food twist that brings people together.
