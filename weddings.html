FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Wedding Catering FAQs

How far do you travel?

We cater across Northamptonshire and the surrounding counties. If your venue is further afield, please get in touch and we'll be happy to discuss your requirements.

Can you cater for vegetarians and dietary requirements?

Yes. We offer vegetarian options and can accommodate most dietary requirements when discussed during the planning stage.

Do you provide serving staff?

Yes. Depending on your package and guest numbers, our friendly, professional team will prepare and serve your food throughout your event.

When should we book?

We recommend booking as early as possible to secure your preferred date, especially during the busy wedding season.
